Questions

What people ask before they book

Smell gas?Call the National Gas Emergency Service free on 0800 111 999. Open doors and windows, and do not use light switches or naked flames.
Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes. Our Gas Safe registration number is 977033, and anyone can check it on the Gas Safe Register.

Do you take on domestic work as well as commercial?

Yes. Commercial and industrial gas is our main line of work, and we also fit and repair boilers, cookers, fires and heating systems in homes.

How is a commercial job priced?

We find out what is needed, then give you the price before we start. Nothing gets fixed without your say-so.

What does a landlord gas safety certificate cost?

From £70 a property. Sample price

How often does a rented home need a gas safety check?

Every 12 months. Your tenants must get a copy of the record within 28 days, and you keep it for two years.

How often should a boiler be serviced?

Once a year. Most manufacturers also require a yearly service to keep the warranty in place.
