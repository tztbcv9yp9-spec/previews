Common questions

Before you ask

How long does an installation take?

A single-room system is usually fitted in a day. Several rooms or a commercial space takes longer, and the quote tells you exactly how long.

Can air conditioning really heat my home?

Yes. Almost every modern system heats as well as cools, and many people use it as their main heating in rooms the radiators never quite reach.

How often does it need servicing?

Once a year for a home system is the usual advice. Busy shops, salons and kitchens benefit from a check every six months.

Will there be much mess?

One small hole through the wall for the pipework, trunking run neatly outside, and everything cleaned up before we leave.
