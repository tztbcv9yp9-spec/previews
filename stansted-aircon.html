The same unit heats the room in winter

Modern air conditioning is a heat pump. It moves heat instead of making it, so one unit on the wall cools the room in summer and warms it in winter. You do not need a separate heater.

What it costs to run depends on the model and how you use it. Ask us for the maker's figures for the unit on your quote.

Why F-Gas matters

Ask every installer you speak to for their certificate number.

Required by law

The systems hold refrigerant gas, so the company and the engineer must both be certified to install or service them.

Needed for the warranty

A maker can turn down a warranty claim if a certified firm did not install the system.

Paperwork at handover

You should be given a commissioning record and the warranty registration. Keep them with the house documents.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Starting prices are in the table further up. After the free survey you get one fixed price in writing, with any extras listed on it.

Will it heat the room in winter?

Yes. Almost every modern system heats as well as cools, and many people use it as the main heating in a room the radiators never quite reach.

How long does fitting take?

It depends on the number of rooms and the length of the pipe run. Your written quote tells you how long yours will take.

Where do the two units go?

The indoor unit sits high on a wall. The outdoor unit goes on brackets or a ground stand, joined by pipework inside white trunking. We agree both positions at the survey.

What if the indoor unit is on an inside wall?

It can usually be done. The unit then needs a small pump to take the water away, and that adds to the price. It will be on your quote.

Is there much mess?

One small hole through the wall for the pipework, trunking run neatly outside, and everything cleaned up before we leave.

How often should it be serviced?

Once a year is the usual advice for a home. Busy shops, salons and kitchens benefit from a check every six months.

Do I need planning permission?

That depends on the property and where the outdoor unit sits. We check at the survey and tell you before you agree to anything.
