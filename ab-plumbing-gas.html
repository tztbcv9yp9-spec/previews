Questions

Asked before most jobs

Smell gas? Call the National Gas Emergency Service free on 0800 111 999. Open doors and windows, and do not use light switches or naked flames.
How much is a boiler service?

£85, with the flue gases checked on an analyser. Sample price

How often should a boiler be serviced?

Once a year. Most manufacturers also require a yearly service to keep the warranty in place.

How long does a new boiler take to fit?

A like-for-like swap is usually done in a day. Moving the boiler or changing the type of system takes longer, and the quote tells you how long.

Do you take on small plumbing jobs?

Yes. Dripping taps, toilets that will not stop running, a radiator that stays cold, a leak under the sink.

Can you do a landlord gas safety check?

Yes. You get the certificate once the appliances have been checked, and a reminder before next year's is due.
