Questions

Before you come in

Do I need to book?

No. We're walk-ins only, so there is nothing to book. Turn up any day of the week and take a seat.

When are you open?

Monday to Thursday 9am to 6pm, Friday 9am to 7pm, Saturday 8.30am to 7pm and Sunday 10am to 5pm.

Do you do hot towel shaves?

Yes. We do hot towel razor shaves and hot towel facial masks, as well as cuts, fades and beard trims.

Where can I park?

Customers tell us there is free parking close to the shop. We're at number 39 on the High Street.
